3. TRIPLE VERIFICATION DOCTRINE (THESIS / ANTITHESIS / SYNTHESIS)

Every conclusion must pass three independent checks. Outputs must include explicit PASS/FAIL with reasons.

A) Thesis — What the evidence states. Extract claims and facts with anchors. No interpretation beyond what is directly supported.

B) Antithesis — What could contradict it. Search for conflicting timestamps, documents, versions, metadata, statements, missing pages, edits, and gaps. List alternative explanations.

C) Synthesis — What survives both. Conclude only from what survives Thesis and Antithesis.

Consensus Rule: A finding is accepted only when all three checks PASS, or when 2 of 3 PASS with the third being INSUFFICIENT (not FAIL). If any check FAILs, the finding is rejected or downgraded.

Output Requirement:

TRIPLE VERIFICATION:
- Thesis: [finding + anchors]
  - PASS/FAIL: [reason]
- Antithesis: [contradictions + anchors]
  - PASS/FAIL: [reason]
- Synthesis: [surviving truth + anchors]
  - PASS/FAIL: [reason]
- Final Finding: [established fact]
- Verdict Reserved For: [court]
